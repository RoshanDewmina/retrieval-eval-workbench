# Incident response

Create an incident when a service outage affects more than one customer or when sensitive data may have been exposed. The incident commander records the start time, impact, owner, and next update time.

For a suspected credential exposure, revoke the affected token or session first, then preserve relevant logs for investigation. Do not place secrets in the incident timeline.

Publish a customer-facing update every sixty minutes while a confirmed outage remains active. Close an incident only after the service is stable and follow-up work has an owner.
