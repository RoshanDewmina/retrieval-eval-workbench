# Roles and access review

The Viewer role can search cases and download approved reports. Viewers cannot change case ownership, export raw attachments, or edit routing rules.

The Operator role can update assigned cases and create redacted exports. The Administrator role manages users, roles, retention settings, and routing rules.

Every quarter, an Administrator must review active Operator and Administrator accounts. Disable accounts for people who no longer need access and record the review date in the access console.
